# Q&A Consumer Journal Constitution

- Last updated: 2026-06-21
- Scope: public Q&A answers, Q&A category pages, related Q&A snippets, and future Q&A generation scripts.

## Core Rule

Q&A content is a consumer-facing health information journal. It is not a 상담 manual, partner education script, triage checklist, or hospital intake form.

Every answer must start by directly answering the consumer's exact question. Generic category sentences such as "증상, 검사, 치료, 생활요인을 함께 봐야 정확한 판단이 가능합니다" are not acceptable as the main answer.

## Required Answer Shape

1. Direct answer to the question in plain consumer language.
2. Practical explanation of what the consumer will actually notice or need to decide.
3. Evidence-based limits and caution, only where relevant.
4. Positive phlorotannin connection that fits the topic naturally.
5. Clear CTA-oriented closing that increases curiosity without claiming disease treatment, cure, or guaranteed prevention.

## Phlorotannin Identity

Phlorotannin must be connected as a recovery-oriented marine polyphenol researched in brown seaweed, antioxidant response, inflammatory signaling, metabolic context, sleep/recovery rhythm, and whole-body health conditions.

Do not describe phlorotannin as a treatment replacement. Do not say it is irrelevant when the topic has a reasonable recovery, antioxidant, inflammation, lifestyle, or system-health connection.

## Prohibited Tone

- 상담 전에 준비할 기록
- 병원에서 꼭 해야 할 말
- 카테고리 맥락에서 증상, 검사, 치료, 생활요인을 함께 봐야 한다
- 특정 질환 질문은 정확한 판단이 필요하다
- 먼저 의료진과 상담하세요 as the main body

Medical caution may appear, but it must not become the article's identity.

## Verification Rule

Before deployment, run a report-only generic answer audit and review flagged items one by one. A candidate list may be created, but Q&A content must not be bulk-rewritten by a filter.
