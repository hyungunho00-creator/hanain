# AI Consumer Cancer Diet Constitution

Created: 2026-06-10

This doctrine applies to all consumer-facing cancer diet blog posts on phlorotannin.com.

## Core Rules

- Cancer diet posts are not partner training material. Write for ordinary readers who want to know what to eat today.
- Titles must use consumer search language, such as "위암에 도움되는 식습관" and "대장암에 도움되는 식습관".
- Do not expose internal words such as partner education, SEO intent, assetization plan, keyword design, or sales strategy inside public body copy.
- Every cancer-specific post must include credible references from WCRF/AICR, NCI, ACS, CDC, ATA, MD Anderson, PubMed, PMC, or equivalent medical institutions.
- Start with food habits first: vegetables, whole grains, legumes, protein, hydration, sodium, alcohol, processed meat, body weight, and treatment-stage cautions.
- Put health supplements after food habits. A supplement paragraph must help readers compare ingredients, timing, transparency, and fit with their current health status.
- Phlorotannin must be positioned strongly and naturally. Use phrases such as 감태 유래 해양 폴리페놀, 항산화 루틴, 프리미엄 해양 폴리페놀, and 왜 더 알아볼 만한 성분인지.
- Do not promise cancer treatment, cancer prevention, recurrence prevention, cure, tumor shrinkage, or anticancer efficacy.
- The right tone is: "플로로탄닌은 암 치료제가 아니라, 식습관과 회복 컨디션을 더 깊게 보게 만드는 항산화 해양 폴리페놀 소재다."
- The copy should make readers curious enough to ask about phlorotannin without using aggressive purchase commands.

## Cancer-Specific Anchors

- Stomach cancer: salt-preserved foods, salty soup, pickled foods, soft protein, cooked vegetables, stomach comfort.
- Colorectal cancer: dietary fiber, whole grains, legumes, vegetables, processed meat reduction, bowel rhythm.
- Liver cancer: alcohol reduction, body weight, safe food storage, aflatoxin awareness, coffee evidence, protein.
- Kidney cancer: body weight, blood pressure, sodium reduction, hydration rhythm, protein balance.
- Thyroid cancer: iodine timing, low-iodine diet before radioactive iodine therapy, seaweed caution, protein, treatment-stage distinction.
- Future cancer posts must define a similarly specific food anchor before mentioning phlorotannin.

## Reader-First Structure

1. Begin with the practical food question the reader actually has.
2. Explain the strongest evidence in plain language.
3. Give five immediately usable table habits.
4. Discuss health foods and supplements cautiously but usefully.
5. Make phlorotannin desirable through curiosity, differentiation, and ingredient identity.
6. End with a family-friendly sentence that sounds like a real person would say it.
